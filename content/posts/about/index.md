Hi, I'm Sully.

I'm an IT consultant for small businesses, where I look after their accounts, devices and networks.

Outside of tech, I like learning about science and history. One of my favorite fun facts is about time: we count in tens because we have ten fingers, but a day has 24 hours because the ancient Egyptians split the day and the night into 12 hours each, possibly because you can count to 12 on the finger joints of one hand using your thumb. Minutes and seconds come in sixties because of the Babylonians, who counted in base 60.

When I'm not doing any of that, I'm usually out on my bike or somewhere outdoors. I also enjoy traveling and finding new music.
